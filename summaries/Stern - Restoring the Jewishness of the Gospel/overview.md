Stern's central thesis is that whilst the Church's proclamation of God's grace to individuals represents authentic Gospel truth, it remains incomplete without restoring the Gospel's Jewish character. The book contends that unless the Church actively works to recover this Jewishness, it lacks a crucial Gospel component, with consequences extending beyond Jewish believers. Written primarily for non-Jewish Christians and Jewish believers in Jesus who have not deeply considered their faith's Jewish dimensions, the work systematically examines how centuries of neglect have obscured Gospel aspects that would have been self-evident to first-century believers.
